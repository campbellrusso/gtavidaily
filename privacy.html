Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

GTA VI DAILY ("the app") is a personal tool. Only its owner uses it to post videos to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

What the app accesses

  • The access tokens each platform gives when the owner signs in, so the app can upload videos to the owner's account.
  • Basic profile information about the owner's own account, such as the username, so the app can confirm it is posting to the right account.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data from any other users or viewers.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking, and it has no analytics.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private file that is never shared or published. The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings.

Deleting data

To remove the app's access, revoke it in your TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings. All locally stored tokens can be deleted by the owner at any time.
